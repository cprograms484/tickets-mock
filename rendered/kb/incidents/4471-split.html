  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day first tenants see day and month flip. “7 April went out on 4 July.”
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as told. The form reads the day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants.
  • move. The date moves by one day, not a flip. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” This looks like a time zone bug. The desk listed these reports in desk-notes.json.

How we counted

  1. The export repeats some reports, because its pages overlap. We count each report id once.
  2. We place a report by its tenant and the way it came in (causes.ts), not by its intake tag. Intake guesses the tag from the browser language, so many tags point to the wrong problem.
  3. A report the desk read as a one-day move counts under move.

Why it was one ticket

Triage merges reports by subject line (decision 0001). All three problems arrive with the same few subject lines, and triage never reads the body. So they landed in one ticket.
